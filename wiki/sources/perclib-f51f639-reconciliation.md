## Scope and provenance

The shared analysis header attributes the collection to earlier private-library proofs. These are relocations and removal of duplicate proofs, not four newly proved wiki assertions. Positive lower bounds on the reciprocal domain and positive field in the infinite-volume exponential remain necessary. The inverse-square-root sum includes n=0, with both sides zero.

The finite-volume header describes correspondence wrappers over the imported mathematical modules. The newly exported 7BMQ, U32Y, SFLE and B12T assertions retain the distinctions between an induced domain and an ambient graph with closed exterior bonds, deterministic identities and measurable-event conclusions, and finite-measure continuity from above versus unrestricted monotone convergence. The construction examples include countable domains inside uncountable ambient space, a root retained by an empty edge set, an excursion outside an observation window, and an all-open ray separating the two orders of limits. The six older wrappers keep their former statements.

The non-diagonal exhaustion condition suffices because the open graph discards loops. Nothing conditions on every exterior bond being closed. Infinite expected volumes remain valid limits in the extended nonnegative reals. The source-level tag annotations are present; this reading alone does not supply an independently reviewed prose correspondence or update the preview's formal-status manifest.
