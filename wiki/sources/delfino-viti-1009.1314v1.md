# The bulk three-point prediction

Read sections1–2 and Table1 of [Delfino–Viti, arXiv:1009.1314v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1009.1314v1) on17 November2026; journal DOI10.1088/1751-8113/44/3/032001.

The full-plane prediction uses CFT and an imaginary-DOZZ structure constant. It must not be conflated with the half-plane boundary constant. [R9NU](../articles/adaptive-three-point.md#section-bulk-factorization) records the later rigorous triangular-site result, with [its own source map](sources/ang-cai-sun-wu-2107.01788v4.md). That does not prove universality for square-lattice bonds.

The Potts and off-critical formulas remain outside this reading. No source fault is asserted, and no derivation in this paper is presented as a completed wiki proof.
