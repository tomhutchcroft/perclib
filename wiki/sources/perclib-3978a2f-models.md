## Scope and provenance

The root-mean example proves HRS7 for an independent probability profile with deterministic path edges, an infinite-degree root and no transitivity. It is not presented as a finite-intensity family or a counterexample under a uniform susceptibility hypothesis. The shift from source index k>=0 to article index k>=1 only relabels the components. Countability is needed to impose all probability-one edge conditions simultaneously.

The finite-range file proves the analytic IXEZ comparison and the geometric and conditional-tail parts of YVJT. It assumes the weighted exponential integrability explicitly before using real integrals. It does not supply the uniform-susceptibility volume-tail theorem used in the article. In the conditional exit statement, M is any real number: M=0 is interpreted using totalized division and yields a constant upper bound, not decay; negative M is equivalent to its absolute value. The percolation application has M>=1. The lattice norm here is the coordinate supremum norm.

The asymptotic file proves the analytic Catalan equivalents, the uniform ratio and joint regime, and connects the critical formulas to the actual binary-tree law. The algebraic Catalan tail at n=0 is two; the volume-tail identity is asserted only for n>=1. Near-critical formulas remain unconditional finite-size probabilities, with a possible infinite atom on the supercritical side. The logarithmic remainder is controlled after multiplication by n, which explains the additional joint-limit condition.

All three headers identify new project proofs. The asymptotic file explicitly uses pinned mathlib's Stirling formula and Catalan numbers. No external-paper error is asserted by this reconciliation.
