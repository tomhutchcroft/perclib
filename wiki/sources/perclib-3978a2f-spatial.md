## Normalization, locality and provenance

The homogeneous-integral formula uses an arbitrary norm and additive Haar measure. The unit-ball integral is retained, so no normalized Euclidean sphere-area constant is silently substituted. Nontriviality makes the origin a null set in the weighted formula; the values assigned to a negative power at zero therefore do not affect it. The formula holds in the nonnegative extended reals even when the unit-ball integral is infinite. Its header attributes the port to yujinhkim/lrp at d382e4c28d92dd4e698ebf4f2aaf0bc932ae73fc, identifying three files and their blobs and declaring Apache-2.0. The earlier upstream revision is explicitly not independently verified. Its witness is the one-dimensional weight P=1 with s=1/2.

The hierarchical source proves shell-sum consequences under pointwise bounds, not those pointwise percolation bounds themselves. In particular, its local-connection result supplies only a lower bound for the full cluster intersection: the direction of local-to-full comparison does not give a full-model upper bound. The existing frozen critical article is unchanged. The source's witness is an explicit radial kernel, rather than a bond model realizing the two-sided critical inputs.

The soft-cutoff source's two row theorems use the positive-part embedding of real numbers into nonnegative extended reals. Their hypotheses alone permit signed radial functions; only the separate nonnegative, monotone cutoff comparison gives the intended unmodified percolation intensities. Its power-law witnesses check the row bound and the cutoff's nonnegativity. Both model files identify the proofs as new project work.
