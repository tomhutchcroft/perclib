## Scope and provenance

The truncated-mean source attributes new proofs to PROVER-G. It uses the proved rooted universal tail to retain half the mean under the cap 99M, then scales down to arbitrary caps. This is a capped variable, not the variable multiplied by an indicator of being below the cap. The averaged lower bound needs no transitivity; passing to one root requires bijections preserving both the law and the observation window. Global transitivity alone does not meet that last hypothesis. The singleton witness has X=1 and M=2.

The moment-ratio header records adaptation of yujinhkim/lrp, branch critical-lrp-1, commit d382e4c28d92dd4e698ebf4f2aaf0bc932ae73fc, files Tightness/MomentRatio.lean (blob prefix 331d362155) and Tightness/RealExponent.lean (e177d7bd87). It records contributor yujinhkim, Apache-2.0 and upstream commit 3b3d1d0e43ef940bfaa2292ee5c2ee094ac4edbf, explicitly not independently verified. The adaptation uses our D-2 window and quantile definitions and, under D-33, the proved rooted universal-tightness theorem; outside quantile existence and monotonicity proofs are not ported. The integer comparison has an explicit factorial constant; the real comparison transfers to a real representative and uses the previously read gamma integral. Almost-sure finiteness is an explicit hypothesis of that transfer, supplied pointwise for finite-window intersections. The maximum lower bound uses M-1 exactly. The zero-power case uses probability normalization and is separate from the positive-base-exponent proof.

The quantile-moment header records adaptation from the same fork and commit, files Tightness/LemmaI26Averaging.lean (817aa6d768) and Analysis/LatticeBallAsymptotic.lean (245577addf), with the same contributor, license and unverified upstream reference. It uses the repository's integer quantile and mathlib sup norm. Counting a large trace in the radius-n ball forces many large rooted traces in translated radius-2n balls; translations then average those tails. The doubling is part of the proof and statement. Paths are in the full ambient model, not restricted to either observation window. No finite-susceptibility or finite-cluster assumption is used. The zero kernel on the two-dimensional lattice and a nonzero nearest-neighbor line supply witnesses. These two provenance paragraphs report merged source headers; no outside-branch code was executed and the outside history has not been independently audited.

The ghost-transform source attributes new proofs to PROVER-F. The tail series and the response-over-field limit use nonnegative integrals and permit arbitrary measures and infinite mean. The response itself converges to the mass of infinite volume under a probability measure. Its two-sided tail equivalence is a comparison theorem for 0<alpha<1, not an exact asymptotic Tauberian theorem and not an alpha=1 statement. The source keeps the m+1 remainder in the finite tail-sum bound. The differential-inequality conclusion uses local absolute continuity on positive compact intervals and monotonicity; it integrates from epsilon>0 and then lets epsilon decrease to zero. There is no assumed value at zero and no division by m. Source comments mention 8B62, 0NVH, 3C2F and CVCN, but no new compiled associations are inferred. Infinite constant volume, the explicit power-tail variable and a square-root response furnish endpoint and nontrivial witnesses.
