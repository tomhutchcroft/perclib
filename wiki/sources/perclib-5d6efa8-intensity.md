## Scope and endpoints

The law-level sprinkling identity, measurable increasing-event monotonicity, positive rescaling and fixed-coordinate profile facts do not assume countability. The two subcritical zero-measure conclusions follow from the definition of infimum even when the underlying event has not been proved measurable. The global-to-root threshold inequality is also a set-inclusion and infimum argument. The article uses ordinary probabilities in its countable setting, and explains the arbitrary-vertex reading through outer measure where needed. It does not infer measurability from the mere use of a measure symbol.

The source uses preconnectedness of the support graph: every pair of vertices is joined by a finite path. This is vacuous on the empty vertex set, whose global event is empty; the rooted assertions additionally supply a root. A singleton has infinite threshold. Positive support connects the positivity conditions at different roots, without asserting equality of their probability values or uniqueness of an infinite cluster. At intensity zero all coordinates are closed; for positive intensity the support is exactly that of the kernel.

The profile and positive-scaling identities need finite nonnegative intensities and a strictly positive finite multiplier. The zero multiplier is treated separately, including when the original threshold is zero. Infinite thresholds remain infinite under positive finite rescaling. No statement here decides percolation at a critical parameter. The large-intensity theorem concerns each fixed pair and does not prove a finite percolation threshold, as the nearest-neighbour line example shows.

The article extends full-profile kernel comparison to off-diagonal comparison by loop erasure, derives the two-sided bound for multiplicatively comparable kernels, and deduces connection of a fixed finite vertex set at large intensity. These are additional expository arguments, not additional exports of the three modules. Its five tags are prose identities pending compiled association and correspondence review. No published-source error was identified; the errata section is unchanged.

The support-component and threshold consequences already appear in [the earlier zero-one article](../articles/infinite-cluster-zero-one.md#section-positive-paths), under C3OO and 1C8G. The companion links them explicitly. Its quantitative survival comparison retains the full two-point probability instead of the probability of one chosen path; the qualitative root-independence consequence is not a newly uncovered gap.
