## Hypotheses and additional deductions

The cut at j forbids every pair with smaller endpoint at most j and larger endpoint strictly greater than j. It is an infinite-coordinate event, not a finite cylinder. The source's finite-cluster conclusion is conditional on two specified bounding cuts; deterministic trapping works after translating the root from zero to any integer.

The source parametrizes crossing pairs by a dependent pair (n,i), with i in Fin n. The n=0 fiber is empty. Thus the length-product's n=0 factor is one even when p(0)=1, and the certainty obstruction must have positive length. The article writes products from n=1 to avoid concealing that convention. Injection is necessary because unordered pairs must not be counted twice.

QH7Q supplies the general closed-coordinate product for a countable injected family, without requiring countability of the ambient coordinate type. The integer-line application is countable automatically. The source's homogeneous profile includes a common diagonal p(0); the article permits arbitrary diagonal marginals because no crossing pair is diagonal. Its arbitrary-profile product statement needs no stationarity.

BQ44 combines the product identity, positivity under the weighted summability/no-certainty hypotheses, and both zero-probability obstructions. A product over independent edge coordinates is used only within one cut. Distinct cuts share coordinates and are not asserted independent.

ZH13 adds an almost-sure supply of cuts by WUAF: positive probability and ergodicity make the stationary cut set nonempty, while stationarity excludes a largest or smallest cut. This is an additional deduction, not an export of CutPoints. The intensity-specific result JK7V already exists and is preserved; its covariance proof is linked rather than rewritten. The profile p(n)=1/(4n^2) is a separate subcritical example with no cuts, showing why zero cut probability is not a percolation criterion.

The zero profile, a certain nearest-neighbour profile, geometric decay with an always-open diagonal, and the empty configuration occur as anonymous witnesses in the source. They are excluded from the theorem count. The article retains the beta=0 endpoint when crossing intensity is infinite. No new published-source diagnosis is made, and all errata are unchanged.
