## Scope and preservation

The first fourteen geometry contracts hold for an arbitrary Abelian coefficient group, with finiteness added only for the finite-ball conclusion. BW7V gives the support criterion, subgroup/coset structure, restriction bijection and disjoint child partition. The existing 9408 and 6KJQ already supply the finite-coordinate-group counts and radial grouping. The child indexed by a fixes coordinate n to a; labels are distinct even when the coefficient group is infinite.

For the model A=(Z/LZ)^d, finite ball and positive-shell counts require L nonzero. The zero shell remains a singleton even when L=0. The full power-kernel summability equivalence instead requires L>=2 and d>=1. YW7B retains these different scopes and the extended geometric identity for arbitrary r, including zero and infinity. For a singleton coefficient group there are no nonzero displacements, so the deleted radial sum is zero even at an infinite ratio.

The seven earlier model contracts and their existing source record are preserved. The exact-geometry module proves stronger formulas, but they are not retroactively attributed to the earlier module. Its algebraic kernel descent omits the nonzero-modulus assumption; the article's existing zero-modulus paragraph covers that case. The 2C estimate uses the sufficient restriction 2 L^d r<=1. The threshold bounds use an extended inverse, so C=0 yields infinite thresholds.

0K45 applies the separate return-corrected theorem 9L60. An ultrametric comparison makes one of the two radial factors at most the direct radial intensity. Summing gives the convolution constant 2 beta M, while the row constant is beta M. No critical behavior is inferred. The source upper bound holds for every finite nonnegative beta and extended M, with r<=1; finite decay is asserted only when M is finite and 2 beta M<1.

The source fixes all pair intensities, including a canonical radial diagonal. The article's arbitrary-diagonal extension follows by loop erasure. Its direct-edge lower comparison is an additional deduction, not a new source export. Zero intensity, zero amplitude, zero ratio, singleton geometry and infinite off-diagonal mass are kept distinct. The original critical hierarchical-two-point article is byte-preserved. No new published-source diagnosis is made, and all errata remain unchanged.
