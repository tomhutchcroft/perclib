## Deterministic scope and endpoint measures

The certificate algebra works without a probability law or countability. The sure event is an identity for arbitrary events only when the coordinate type is finite. For an infinite coordinate type, the event that every coordinate is open has no finite certificate and hence has empty disjoint occurrence with the sure event. Triple associativity and union distribution here require increasing events and use the finite open-witness form.

Cylinder restriction is different: it preserves and reflects disjoint occurrence for arbitrary events, with no increasingness, finiteness or countability assumption on either coordinate set. To restrict a forcing certificate, extend each test configuration by the original background outside the restricted set. This preserves agreement on the whole original certificate. Injectivity of the subtype inclusion preserves finiteness when the actual disjoint-occurrence certificates are restricted.

The zero-one product profile is a Dirac measure on the product sigma-algebra for any coordinate set. On an uncountable set this does not assert measurability of the singleton containing its deterministic configuration. Finite-event indicator formulas and deterministic BK need no increasingness. The witnesses include an empty index type, distinct and repeated indispensable coordinates, and a mixed zero-one profile on two coordinates with arbitrary events.

## The sign in the induction

Write Cij for disjoint occurrence of the i and j sections. The open section is bounded by the union of C10 and C01. Since C00 lies in both, its indicator can be subtracted once. Combining this with the exact closed-section identity gives coefficient 1-2p on C00. The weighted recursion holds for every probability parameter, including p>1/2. Substitution of an upper induction bound for C00 requires 1-2p to be nonnegative, which is precisely the low-density restriction.

The arithmetic remainder p squared times (a1-a0)(b1-b0) is nonnegative for every real p when section values are ordered. Likewise, coordinate reindexing, the finite expectation splitting identity and the empty-coordinate base case are algebraic statements with no probability-range requirement. Monotonicity of finite expectation does require nonnegative configuration weights, and the source assumes 0 <= p <= 1. The actual low-density theorem is homogeneous; the newer profile module supplies inhomogeneous induction, block refinement and endpoint continuity. The [full-profile and countable inventory](sources/perclib-22d8485-bk.md) records those later steps. The source includes p=0, p=1/2 and empty-coordinate witnesses.
