## Scope and provenance

The martingale file proves the finite-horizon L2 bound and the square compensator. It does not yet prove the variance-cutoff stopping construction of HXNR, the arbitrary-time limiting step or the damped maximum theorem. Its L2 and compensator theorems assume a finite measure, not necessarily total mass one; the absolute-value submartingale lemma has no finite-measure premise. The compensator starts at zero, so for a martingale with nonzero initial value the expectation of the square differs from the expected variation by the initial second moment.

The thirteen boundary/forest wrappers package already-proved imported results under existing tags. The full source, including its private susceptibility calculation and non-vacuity witnesses, has been read. Path-event estimates on an arbitrary host do not require off-host bonds to be closed; estimates for the entire cluster do. The row-kernel statements are nonnegative extended sums on arbitrary types, not finite sums or claims that every sequence is a simple path. The frozen subcritical article remains unchanged.

Boundary marks encode the existence of an open bond to the exterior. Distinct marks use disjoint bond coordinates, giving joint independence with internal coordinates, even when exterior endpoints coincide. Collapsing the entire exterior can join originally disjoint components; the final witness explicitly demonstrates this. The internal induced law is a pushforward under restriction, not a conditional law given boundary data. These source tags do not by themselves constitute fingerprint-bound correspondence reviews.
