## Scope and provenance

The Fourier source attributes new proofs to PROVER-F, using the lattice shell bounds and mathlib trigonometric and harmonic estimates. It defines its symbol by the cosine series. Symmetry of the mass function is not assumed in the source; without it, this is the real part of the complex Fourier transform. Laziness is used only to make that cosine symbol nonnegative, not in the small-frequency comparisons. The power bounds use the Euclidean norm, while shell counting uses the sup norm with explicit dimension factors. Their comparison is two-sided up to positive constants, not an exact asymptotic ratio. The lower proof reflects the radial comparison weight, not the possibly asymmetric mass function. The inverse-square witness on the line uses the zeta-value normalization and gives a linear small-frequency deficit. Source comments name VJCW without supplying a new compiled tag association. Green-function inversion, convolution powers and percolation domination are separate from this module.

The infinite-volume Russo header records the premise in the outside proof of Lemma I.4.1 of Critical long-range percolation I, arXiv:2508.18807v1. It cites yujinhkim/lrp, branch critical-lrp-1, commit d382e4c28d92dd4e698ebf4f2aaf0bc932ae73fc, files PercLib/CriticalLRP/RussoInf/DominatedFlux.lean, Exchange.lean, FluxMain.lean, CutoffFlux.lean and Capstone.lean. It records contributor yujinhkim, Apache-2.0 as declared in the file headers and third_party/critical-lrp/NOTICE.md, and upstream yujinhkim/Critical-Long-range-percolation at 3b3d1d0e43ef940bfaa2292ee5c2ee094ac4edbf, explicitly not independently verified. The header states that the argument was read there but no code copied: the expressions use our D-2 interface and the proofs use our finite Russo and dominated-limit results. This inventory records that source attribution; it does not independently audit the outside history, and no outside-branch code was executed.

The essential observable hypothesis is boundedness and eventual constancy as a function of extended cluster cardinality. It includes capped powers and finite cluster-size tail indicators, not unbounded susceptibility itself. The general result first proves an integrated formula: fixed-edge pivotal expectations converge by bounded convergence, the touching bound dominates the edge sum, and a uniform bound then dominates the parameter integral. The union of the coordinate exceptional sets is countable. Continuity of the infinite flux is proved separately for the cutoff family before inferring the ordinary derivative away from lattice-distance corners. A single upper scale with finite susceptibility supplies the dominator at lower scales. That finite-susceptibility assumption remains to be established in an application; neither absence of infinite clusters alone nor boundedness of the observable replaces it. No new statement is made at a critical parameter with infinite susceptibility or after removing an observable cap. The explicit lattice witness has K(s)=s^(-2), beta=1/10, upper radius 5/2 and a cluster-size-at-least-two indicator; the differentiability witness uses radius 7/4. A two-vertex model also witnesses the general integrated theorem.
