## Scope and proof correspondence

The finite edge witness need not be a tree, loop-free or locally finite as an ambient graph. Graph construction discards loops. The source inducts on its finite edge count, deleting all edges at the current root. It either moves to a component containing every target or splits targets between components. If a target coincides with the root, it is split off with an empty edge witness. Labels, rather than target positions, are partitioned, so repeated positions remain valid. A prescribed label selects the orientation of the two groups before the union bound. The vertex type need only be countable in the probability theorem; the index type is arbitrary with a finite selected label set.

The source uses finite open witnesses for increasing connection events and applies countable profile BK twice to their nested disjoint occurrence. It imports reachability helpers from the earlier TreeSplitting module; this inventory does not claim a complete read of that separate module or its marked-piece splitting theorem. The probability source exports the three-point L82Q specialization and the recursive group bound, not a full sum indexed by trivalent shapes for every fixed terminal list. The earlier AFH6 shape formula remains a separate wiki argument.

The moment tuple identities count ordered tuples with repeated entries. Pointwise identities work for arbitrary vertex types and use extended nonnegative sums. Countability supplies measurability and the countable Tonelli step for expectations. The complementary-group factorization sums separate target coordinates; it does not assume that the two connection events in the same configuration are independent. Its pointwise form uses two arbitrary configurations, and its probability form can be obtained by two successive integrations. Empty groups and order zero are allowed there. The recursion itself requires order at least two and two nonempty parts, so induction always uses smaller positive orders.

The source proves the coefficient recursion through Catalan identities. The article also derives it by splitting the labelled trivalent shapes already counted by SO5J. The source's natural subtraction makes 2m−3 equal zero at m = 1, so its coefficient is 0!! = 1; the mathematical notation (-1)!! = 1 expresses the same initial value. No negative natural number is being evaluated. The exported bound allows any nonnegative extended majorant M, while the article's useful moment estimate assumes a finite uniform majorant. For M infinite and order at least two, the bound is trivial because root susceptibility is at least one. At order one it is the susceptibility identity, with exponent zero.

Anonymous witnesses include the three-point bound on two vertices and on the integers, a three-target finite-profile recursion with a repeated root target, the first-moment identity, the second-moment bound on two vertices, and the third-moment coefficient three on the integers. All profiles may include endpoint probabilities and arbitrary diagonal marginals.

The module headers cite Aizenman–Newman (1984) as motivation without exact theorem locators, and cite the earlier wiki statements.
