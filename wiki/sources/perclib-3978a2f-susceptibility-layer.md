The source supplies the finite-volume and regularity parts of TAVM/MXGM, the independent-layer ghost comparison BEJT, the susceptibility extension MLLO, and the quantitative right-openness and threshold lower bound in 5LNL. The endpoint blow-up clauses of MXGM and 5LNL are not exports of these files. This is a source-inspected correspondence, not compiled tag-association evidence or an independent review.

## Arguments and limits

Finite-volume differentiation precedes exhaustion. On a compact interval of finite susceptibility, monotonicity bounds the full susceptibility squared and hence makes the dominating integral finite. The reciprocal estimate can then be obtained by summing a quadratic-error step bound over equal subdivisions; this proof does not need to differentiate the reciprocal. Almost-everywhere differentiability is a separate consequence of local Lipschitz continuity. Neither conclusion asserts finiteness of susceptibility at an endpoint.

The ghost comparison is realized on three independent coordinate blocks. For a fixed finite red cluster, its value is determined by red pairs meeting it, while the outside union cluster uses only pairs wholly outside it. The blue boundary pair and the marks inside the cluster form two more disjoint coordinate sets. This proves the exact factorization without conditioning on a possibly null event. A last exit, rather than an arbitrary exit, supplies an outside continuation.

For MLLO the source first obtains a ghost bound proportional to h. Since an infinite cluster hits positive-density marks almost surely, that bound forces zero probability of an infinite union cluster. Only then does the source use Fatou with T exp(-hT), whose value at infinite T is zero under the extended arithmetic convention. The article's direct monotone-convergence argument with (1-exp(-hT))/h also handles infinite T, and remains valid. These are two proofs of the same conclusion, not a correction to the existing theorem or an erratum in a paper.

The intensity source includes zero-row kernels, for which every proper bond is closed and susceptibility is one. Its extended threshold bound then reads infinity <= b_chi. The zero-kernel examples therefore witness right-openness and the threshold lower bound but do not witness a finite critical threshold. The derivative file uses the two-vertex model to witness its regularity hypotheses; it does not claim endpoint blow-up in that finite model. Existing lattice and long-range endpoint arguments remain separate.
