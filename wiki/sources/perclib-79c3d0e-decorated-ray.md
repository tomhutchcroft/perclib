## Scope and correspondence

The source uses all natural-number pairs as vertices. Its existential host is the entire comb, with a ray above every backbone vertex. The intensity support retains only the first 2^k vertical edges at k >= 1, leaving the other vertices isolated. The host is connected, but the positive-intensity graph on the entire vertex type is not. Restricting to the root's positive-intensity component gives exactly the earlier wiki construction. The susceptibility threshold is asserted at the chosen root; the source does not assert the same threshold at its isolated vertices.

The support hypothesis in the path-product and cluster-finiteness statements excludes positive diagonal intensities as well as all other non-model pairs. Graph conclusions can be extended to arbitrary diagonals by erasure, but that is not an exported hypothesis of these statements. The elementary closure lemma uses raw pair membership and is stronger than closure under non-loop graph adjacency. The zero-intensity susceptibility statement retains a support premise although the general zero-intensity fact needs none.

Finiteness at every root uses the unit backbone and finite-decoration support, without the particular pendant weights. Only the susceptibility calculation needs the k^2 weights and exact zeros. Below log 2 the source bounds the sum by two geometric series. At or above log 2, Bernoulli's inequality bounds the chance of traversing a whole decoration below by 3/4 for k >= 2; its contribution to susceptibility is at least that much. The source uses the weaker constant 1/2 to conclude divergence. The wiki gives the sharper elementary lower bound without attributing it to a separate exported declaration.

The symmetric kernel is constructed explicitly in the existence proof. The degree bound and connectedness of the comb are proved in the existential witness. The anonymous example combines susceptibility one at zero with the full strict-threshold equivalence. Thresholds are extended nonnegative infima over finite parameters; no probability law at infinity is invoked. Almost-sure all-cluster finiteness is asserted separately for each finite beta, without a simultaneous coupling over parameters.
