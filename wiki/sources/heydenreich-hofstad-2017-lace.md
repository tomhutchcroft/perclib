The cutting-bond probability identity needs finite clusters, not finite susceptibility. The finite signed expansion also needs finiteness of the sums being subtracted; this is automatic below criticality and stated explicitly in the critical extension. This map does not certify an unrestricted critical expansion from nonpercolation alone. No new source erratum, coefficient bound, infrared theorem or compiled Lean association is claimed.
