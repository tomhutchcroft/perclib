## Hypotheses and proof boundaries

Left continuity is extended-valued and needs no finite-row bound. Lower semicontinuity comes from finite-path approximation and nonnegative summation; monotonicity turns it into a left limit. At parameter zero, the source uses the empty left-neighbourhood filter on the nonnegative half-line, which carries no right-limit information. Infinite susceptibility remains an actual infinite value, not a real conversion.

Boundary-flux convergence instead requires a finite uniform bound on the off-diagonal probability rows and finite susceptibility at one root. It needs no transitivity. The summable majorant is Q times the root two-point function. The exhaustion may omit the root initially; its internal connection events then vanish. The existence form adds the root to the exhausting sets. The intensity corollary uses q_beta <= beta J and a uniform finite intensity-row bound.

For continuity of a fixed finite-set boundary functional, a local uniform majorant B J on a bounded intensity interval controls the exterior sum. Only the rows at vertices in that set enter this argument, although the exported boundary-continuity statement assumes all rows finite. No ambient countability is needed for that continuity statement: internal connections are finite cylinders. Countability is present in the source monotonicity and transport statements and in the threshold conclusions.

The source's kernel automorphisms preserve all unordered-pair weights, including diagonals. Graph conclusions under off-diagonal invariance follow after erasing diagonal coordinates, which changes none of the relevant observables. Root/global threshold equality needs no connectedness of the support. Transport plus continuity makes a strict certificate persist at a larger intensity, and the imported contraction theorem gives finite susceptibility there. This proves the strict threshold comparison and R84Z. These files do not export the full RDF0 equivalence or L0TE density conclusion. The separate BoundaryContraction module is imported here but is not included in this full-file inventory.

The derivative argument uses finite-coordinate Russo and cylinder BK even on an uncountable ambient vertex type. A simple pivotal path has two edge-disjoint parts after removing the pivotal edge. Both orientations of each proper unordered pair are counted once. The exponential closed-edge factor converts unconditional pivotality into closed pivotality before it is bounded by one; it cannot be omitted from the derivative identity itself. Loops do not affect the event. The source uses a nonnegative-part extension to negative real parameters, so the endpoint theorem is a derivative within the nonnegative half-line, not an ordinary derivative at zero. Infinite-volume susceptibility identities and lower derivative estimates require additional arguments and are not exported by this file.

Named witnesses give the nearest-neighbour intensity kernel on the integers, the constant kernel on the two-element cyclic group, and the zero kernel on an arbitrary type. Anonymous examples instantiate left continuity on the line, a contracting set for the zero profile, root independence and boundary continuity on two vertices, persistence for the zero kernel, the zero derivative on a singleton, and the derivative bound on two vertices. The zero-kernel persistence example does not witness the finite-critical-point hypothesis of R84Z: its threshold is infinite. No stronger non-vacuity claim is made here.
