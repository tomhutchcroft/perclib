## Domains, measurability and order of limits

The source internal cluster is empty when the root is outside the induced vertex domain. This is not the singleton cluster obtained by retaining an ambient root while closing all exterior pairs. Increasing domains need not contain the root at their first index, be finite, or have bounded degree or interaction range. The all-vertex constant domain gives a valid deterministic exhaustion on any vertex set. The source natural-number example has root 5 and initial-segment domains, making the initial empty cases explicit.

Countability of a single domain suffices for local connection measurability, without ambient countability. The volume expectation and survival probability theorems are exported with countable ambient vertices. Their expectations may be infinite. The cardinality statements are deterministic and use the extended size, sending every infinite set to infinity.

The final two LocalConnectivity theorems and the escape-supremum theorem in FiniteVolume have no ambient countability assumption. Their measure evaluations are outer measures when event measurability has not been established. 6QN7 explains continuity from below for the outer measure of a finite measure using measurable covers; it does not silently label these expressions measurable-event probabilities. The source's separate finite-set exhaustion identifies survival by a decreasing intersection, followed by an outer infimum over that exhaustion. It does not authorize exchanging this infimum with the domain supremum.

The first-exit decomposition is deterministic on arbitrary domains. Independence of its internal prefix and one specified boundary coordinate requires a countable domain but no ambient countability. The union bound exported for the boundary functional uses a finite domain and countable ambient vertices; the external sum can be infinite. Its weights are the original opening probabilities q, with no intensity substitution or omitted factor. The two source sum formats enumerate the same nonnegative terms. An infinite cluster must leave a finite domain, giving the survival comparison, but no sharpness iteration follows from that one inequality alone.

On the ray, all nonconsecutive nondiagonal probabilities vanish, while diagonal probabilities are arbitrary. Both the escape probability and boundary functional equal t^(n+1), including t=0, t=1 and n=0. The internal zero-length connection has probability one.
