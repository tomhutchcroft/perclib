## Exact equations and model hypotheses

The gyration file solves an exact equation on the entire positive half-line. Its ratio limits allow zero or negative constants and must not be restated as positive asymptotic equivalents without extra hypotheses. It supplies no percolation derivative identity. The header attributes the analytic port to yujinhkim/lrp at d382e4c28d92dd4e698ebf4f2aaf0bc932ae73fc with an Apache-2.0 declaration; its earlier upstream origin is explicitly unverified. The displayed witnesses use the power r^3 and the critical solution r^4(1+log r).

The logarithmic and good-scale files identify their proofs as new project work. The first implements a constant susceptibility/diagram witness and the exact OP8F solution r^3/sqrt(6 log r). The latter witnesses DISO with equality, the exponential-tail bound by a point mass, and good-scale persistence by an everywhere-good example. That persistence witness makes the conditional derivative premise inactive; it does not realize a transition through bad scales in a percolation model.

For good-scale persistence, the last good scale is itself good by monotonicity and continuity of the deterministic comparison power. Continuity of the quantile function is not assumed. The rooted exponential-tail estimate is an input to the finite-measure moment theorem, not a proof of universal tightness. Likewise 6PGV assumes the corrected quadratic inequality and OP8F assumes the deficit difference; neither source supplies those model estimates.
