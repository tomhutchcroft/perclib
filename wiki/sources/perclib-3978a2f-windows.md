## Hypotheses and representation

The deterministic partition formula allows an arbitrary vertex type and an arbitrary additive commutative monoid. The expectation specializations use the countable independent-bond model. The explicit OB0A moment exports have natural orders; real nonnegative orders in the article follow from the general function identity, not from silently changing the type of those exports. A uniform random root requires a nonempty window even though the algebraic average is meaningful by convention on the empty window. LQ6Y does not require universal tightness: partition counting and quantile minimality suffice, and the source includes p=0.

The hyperscaling files assume rooted damping explicitly or through a supplied universal-tightness property. They do not reprove it. UVQ6 uses tails at every root, while MJEP uses the tail only at its chosen root. The DEZI export is a statement about real functions, a natural-indexed tail function and numerical comparisons; it does not construct a random variable having that tail or establish the model-specific matching hypotheses. Its scale tends to infinity, making the ceiling harmless up to a factor two. The 7VCS export assumes existing logarithmic limits and gives inequalities, not equalities.

The splitting combinatorics use finite open witnesses but do not require the whole coordinate type to be finite. The probability identity does require finite coordinates. Both copies have the same opening probability p, with the other coordinates still shared. The second summand vanishes only under the explicit criterion in XWH4; no bound on a sum over split coordinates is proved. The existing article proof is retained without editorial revision. Bubble-only reverse estimates remain with FIXER.

All three headers identify new project proofs. The window files cite Hutchcroft, arXiv:2008.11197v2, section 2, as motivation. This inventory creates no new paper erratum and supplies no confirmation for existing provisional entries.
