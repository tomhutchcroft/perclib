## Scope and remaining correspondence

- The geometry argument includes dimension zero. Positivity of all path counts, the logarithmic limit proof and the percolation consequence assume dimension at least one. In dimension zero the graph is a singleton and every positive-length path count is zero.
- The path count distinguishes paths, not just endpoints. Its submultiplicative injection retains the first segment's endpoint so that the translated second segment can be restored. It does not assert that arbitrary pairs concatenate to self-avoiding paths.
- The source's limit and pointwise bound imply the infimum characterization given in F3YB. The article supplies the quotient-and-remainder proof. The dimension-one witness yields μ = 1; neither source identifies μ in higher dimensions.
- The source's natural subtraction at degree zero is not silently converted into a negative real base. The article states the zero-dimensional counts separately.
- The modules live in the historical site-model namespace but their results are deterministic graph statements. The added percolation corollary explicitly uses independent bonds and the root-containing bond cluster; it does not identify site and bond susceptibilities.
- The geometry source includes anonymous witnesses for dimensions zero, one and two. The path-growth source includes witnesses for μ = 1 in dimension one and the length-three upper bound in dimension two. These witnesses are not added to the named-theorem count.
- The connective-constant header cites Hammersley–Morton (1954) and Madras–Slade (1993), §1.2, with unchecked locators. Those works were not inspected for this batch; exact bibliographic correspondence remains pending. This is a provenance limitation, not evidence of an error in either published source.
