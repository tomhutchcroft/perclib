## Hypotheses and provenance

The Riccati comparisons assume a positive differentiable function on a whole terminal interval; the lower comparison additionally assumes divergence. Their mean-value argument needs no continuous derivative. For the exact equation, the measurable bounded error makes the derivative of the reciprocal locally bounded and the weighted tail absolutely integrable. Boundedness alone does not imply a vanishing relative remainder. The source implements all three cases with f(r)=r, error zero and A=alpha=starting radius=1. Its attribution identifies the yujinhkim/lrp contribution at d382e4c28d92dd4e698ebf4f2aaf0bc932ae73fc, with an Apache-2.0 declaration; the earlier upstream origin remains marked unverified in the header. This reading does not fetch or certify that upstream origin.

The reciprocal and linear files identify their proofs as new project work. The reciprocal file witnesses the power-law comparison by its equality solution and the logarithmic upgrade by the constant function one. Its two-point construction diagnoses a lack of information in moment orders, not a percolation counterexample. The logarithmic result supplies no model-specific derivative estimate, and no reverse-BK conclusion is inferred.

For the linear equation, the implemented asymptotic theorems below and at alpha=2 omit the article's nonnegativity assumption on S; below two they also allow negative alpha. The alpha>2 case retains S>=0, which rules out cancellation of the homogeneous coefficient. The source's derivative equation and absolute continuity already imply local integrability of the forcing, so the lack of a separate premise does not remove that analytic control. The three witnesses are r^3, r^4 log r and r^6-r^5, for alpha=1,2,3 respectively. Identifying any of these equations with cluster observables remains a separate model argument.
