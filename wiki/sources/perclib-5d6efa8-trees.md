## Model conventions and scope

The root is the empty word, children are formed by prepending letters, and ancestors are suffixes. Hence the child subtree indexed by a letter is obtained by appending that letter to every word. This identifies the first edge and all the internal ancestral edges without confusing their coordinate sets. Tree edges always have distinct endpoints.

The deterministic converse statements in Tree use raw configuration containment in the host edge set. GGH2 uses the corresponding open-graph containment version, obtained by erasing loops. The probabilistic tree profile is specified only off the diagonal. Full connection to a depth is measurable by the countable-vertex connection theorem, while existence of an ancestral witness at a fixed depth is already a finite cylinder. They agree on the almost-sure support event but are not asserted identical for unrestricted configurations with off-tree edges.

Finite branching makes every bounded collection of levels finite, which is essential to the infinite-cluster/depth equivalence. The union bound counts at most k^n distinct words and uses n distinct edges on each ancestral chain. Depth zero is certain, including k=0 and p=0; for k=0 the root is the only vertex. No statement about an infinitely branching tree is inferred.

The exported recursion uses the canonical profile with all non-tree pairs, including loops, set to zero. UHVH extends its graph-event interpretation to arbitrary diagonal marginals by LNKT. The pullback map is injective and preserves the canonical profile; the first edge and subtree coordinates are independent, and different children use disjoint coordinate blocks. These use the earlier product-law assertions QH7Q and YMHY.

The scalar contracts are included with their actual hypotheses. In particular, monotonicity allows all real s<=t<=1, whereas the strict contraction statement requires a positive s at most one and p<1. The quadratic bound supplies an explicit positive subsolution when kp>1. The article identifies the decreasing depth limit as the largest fixed point and gives the subsolution argument directly.

The final source criterion requires k>=1 and includes p=1 separately. Omitting that disjunction would incorrectly discard the deterministic ray k=1,p=1. For k>=2 the critical offspring law is nondegenerate and extinction holds at p=1/k. The root susceptibility and critical arm asymptotic in the article are additional deductions from UKY4 and 8WO7, not exports of these two modules. CQYY and NDIC retain the existing binary-tree assertions and constants.

The source includes zero-branching, probability-zero, canonical-profile and arbitrary-diagonal witnesses; they are excluded from the named-theorem count.
