## Definition and deterministic scope

The [susceptibility target definition at L54](https://github.com/tomhutchcroft/perclib/blob/a920a1ec32e38ee69a36bc4d4b81ef6a1ec1de7d/PercLib/Percolation/Bond/Susceptibility.lean) bundles measurable extended volume and the nonnegative two-point sum. GUHT and 8DSI already supply these claims. It is excluded from the theorem count. The deterministic indicator identity allows any vertex type and interprets every infinite cardinality as infinity; Tonelli is used only for the countable probabilistic identity.

Finite-window tails do not restrict the root to the window or paths to the induced graph. Their common finite witness is the union of finitely many finite paths. The source exhaustion covers all pair coordinates, including diagonals, though graph observables ignore loops. Its probability-convergence theorem assumes that exhaustion directly rather than a separate countability instance. This is not an unrestricted theorem for arbitrary uncountable edge sets without a countable exhaustion. Zero thresholds, empty windows, a singleton root in the empty configuration and a genuine two-vertex connection are included.

## Full volume and infinite values

The tail-sum identity counts thresholds n+1, retaining the root contribution. The infinite-cluster probability is the decreasing-tail infimum, whereas susceptibility is their sum. The latter can diverge while the former is zero. Markov is exported without division and includes n=0. The one-edge lower bound is a measure-monotonicity statement even without ambient countability; the article makes no uncountable connection-measurability inference. The row lower bound excludes the diagonal and retains the leading one. The witnesses include singleton susceptibility, an always-open pair, and infinite susceptibility on the all-open natural vertices.

## Quantile scope and the recorded erratum

Attainment, minimality, threshold characterization and the cardinality bound in the source do not assume ambient countability; they use monotonicity of the total measure expression. The article states the probability interpretation on countable vertices. Profile monotonicity and local stability in this module assume finite ambient vertices, not just a finite observation window. The general continuous-parameter statement follows by pulling back the neighbourhood in 0SB0. Empty-window quantile one and singleton quantile two are explicit non-vacuity checks.

The source cites arXiv:2103.17013v1, section 2.1, as motivation and replaces the claimed continuity with left stability following FIXER's resolution. The [existing hierarchical erratum](../errata/hutchcroft-hierarchical-2021.json) records that resolution and implementation; its status is unchanged. The endpoint bound requires positive finite b and a bound at every positive smaller intensity. Stability within the closed left interval also includes b=0; the punctured limit there is vacuous. Neither the helper nor the added prose proves the hierarchical subcritical estimate or finiteness of its critical parameter.
