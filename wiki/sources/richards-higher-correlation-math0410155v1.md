# Richards: the conditioning step

Read the extracted [versioned electronic reprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0410155v1) on 17 November2026. Its pagination differs from the journal's. This audit concerns Theorem1.1 and section2, chiefly electronic-reprint pp.7–9, equations(2.5)–(2.13).

The calculation fixes a one-coordinate fibre; the final substitution requires arbitrary fibres. [ICQP](../articles/adaptive-three-point.md#section-higher-correlation-conditioning) gives the missing conditional decomposition and an explicit negative covariance remainder. This diagnoses an unsupported induction, not a false general inequality. The [source erratum](../errata/richards-higher-correlation-2004.json#conditioning-induction) is provisional pending Tom's confirmation.

Valid replacements are the [positive-cumulation theorem](../articles/adaptive-three-point.md#section-higher-correlation-positive) and the [three-function square theorem](../articles/adaptive-three-point.md#section-two-chain-correlation), each with additional hypotheses. See the [Sahi map](sources/sahi-higher-correlation-2008.md) and [Lieb–Sahi map](sources/lieb-sahi-higher-correlation-2107.09838v2.md).

Sections3–6, the diffusion and duplicate-variable approaches, and the full higher-order claims remain outside this audit. No later-version, visual-PDF, journal-text, compiled correspondence or current-status certification is claimed.
