# Heydenreich–van der Hofstad: intrinsic arms, extrinsic lower bounds and dimension

The remainder of Chapter 11 of the [17 March 2017 author manuscript](https://rhofstad.win.tue.nl/survey-high-d-percolation.pdf), Sections 11.3–11.4, printed pp.139–145 / PDF indices150–156, has been read in extracted text. Together with the [correlation/Fourier map](sources/heydenreich-hofstad-2017-correlation-fourier.md), this completes chapter reading, not every theorem's proof. Visual-page and published-edition checks remain unavailable.
