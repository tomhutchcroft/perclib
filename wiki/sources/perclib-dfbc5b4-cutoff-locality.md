## Scope and provenance

The finite-susceptibility source attributes new proofs to PROVER-G, applying the assembled sharpness theorem. Its lattice power-law specialization uses the standard norm on integer vectors, which here is the sup norm. Its hierarchical specialization retains the sufficient factor-two condition; the general group theorem applies whenever the actual displacement mass is finite. The witnesses include a nonzero nearest-neighbor kernel, a one-dimensional inverse-square kernel and a binary hierarchical kernel. None identifies their critical intensities or proves absence of percolation at criticality.

The cutoff source attributes new proofs to the pinned mathlib integration and differentiation tools. The four target conclusions instantiate the previously read interface. The factor 1/(d+alpha) comes from integrating the radius rate and must not be dropped when comparing the kernel with an unnormalized power law. The kernel is continuous at its distance corner, but its ordinary derivative is asserted only away from that corner. The source’s explicit one-dimensional witness has radius two and distance one, yielding (1-2^(-2))/2.

The omitted-layer header records the outside premise. It cites yujinhkim/lrp, branch critical-lrp-1, commit d382e4c28d92dd4e698ebf4f2aaf0bc932ae73fc, files PercLib/CriticalLRP/Analysis/VolumeTailAllN.lean, VolumeTailUpper.lean and CountableProduct/GhostCoupling.lean, contributor yujinhkim, Apache-2.0, and upstream commit 3b3d1d0e43ef940bfaa2292ee5c2ee094ac4edbf explicitly not independently verified. The header says no code is copied and the proofs are new. This is a record of the merged source’s provenance statement, not an independent audit of the outside history. No outside-branch code was executed.

For the power profile c s^(-d-alpha), the omitted-row constant is c times [(1+2/kappa)^d + kappa^(-d) 2d 3^(d-1) (1+1/alpha)]. Its norm comparison N(z)>=kappa times the sup norm is an explicit hypothesis. Such a comparison follows for a fixed norm inherited from the finite-dimensional real space; it is not automatic for every abstract additive-group norm. The argument dominates the row by a constant on the comparison ball and a sup-norm power tail outside it. The source witness evaluates C=7 in dimension one with c=alpha=kappa=1. The probability ratio has a strictly positive denominator because all intensities are finite. These results discharge the omitted-row premise for this model, not every hypothesis of the cited volume-tail propositions.

The local-convergence source attributes new proofs to PROVER-F. It first transports the finite certificate through an arbitrary injective region map, requiring support coverage on both sides. A graph ball one layer larger than the certificate supplies that support condition for nearest-neighbor bonds. The limiting graph needs connectedness and local finiteness, while transitivity is needed on the approximating graphs to turn a transported contraction into a threshold bound. The conclusion is in the unbounded intensity parameter; conversion to edge probability uses p=1-exp(-beta), with beta=infinity corresponding to p=1. Only lower semicontinuity is proved. The witnesses use identity transport and a constant sequence of integer-line graphs.
