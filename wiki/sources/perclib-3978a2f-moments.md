## What these ingredients establish

Tuple re-rooting is a deterministic bijection of tuples in an additive commutative group. Its extended sums need neither countability nor finite mass. It does not establish invariance of a model, or differentiation and domination for a moment hierarchy. The binomial identities hold for signed real inputs and p=0; their application to cluster sizes requires finite real values or a separate limiting argument.

The factorial file's convolution has second factor (2n-2k-3)!!, and must not be confused with the distinct tree-graph convolution whose second factor is (2(n-k)-1)!!. The proof applies Pascal's rule to a general recurrence before specializing. Its printed n=3 witness checks the exact indices.

The mixed-moment theorem assumes measurability of sums of all nonnegative measurable test functions over the random finite set. Mere measurability of individual membership events on an uncountable space is insufficient. The source allows a general real inner product space and higher moments with infinite values. It writes volume bias and uniform sampling as normalized integrals, without constructing another probability space or asserting independence of position and volume. The singleton witness verifies the assumptions; percolation moment bounds remain inputs.
