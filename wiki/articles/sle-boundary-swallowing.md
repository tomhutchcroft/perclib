## Where a boundary collision occurs {#section-first-hit}

<p>Let $U:[0,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ be continuous with $U_0=0$, and let $g_t:D_t\to\mathbb H$ solve the chordal Loewner equation with coefficient two. Write $f_t=g_t^{-1}$ and let $\tau(x)$ be the maximal lifetime of the real flow started at $x\ne0$. Assume that the chain is generated by a continuous curve $\gamma$, that every $f_t$ extends continuously to the closed half-plane, and that $f_t(U_t)=\gamma(t)$. Put $\Gamma=\gamma[0,\infty)$.</p>

<a id="tag-E5JX" data-assertion="proposition" data-title="A boundary-flow collision identifies the first trace hit and separates every preceding compact interval from the future domain"></a>
<p>Fix $a&gt;0$ with $T=\tau(a)&lt;\infty$. Then $X_a:=\gamma(T)$ is real and at least $a$, and</p>
<div class="math-display">$$X_a=\min\bigl([a,\infty)\cap\Gamma\bigr),\qquad
\tau(b)=T\ \Longleftrightarrow\ a\leq b\leq X_a\quad(b\geq a).$$</div>
<p>The time $T$ is the first time the trace meets $[a,\infty)$. Every compact $I\subset[a,X_a)$ satisfies</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\operatorname{dist}(I,D_T)&gt;0,\qquad
\operatorname{dist}(I,\Gamma)&gt;0.$$</div>

<p><em>Proof.</em> Before $T$, order preservation keeps all real flows started in $[a,\infty)$ alive and to the right of the driver. For $s&lt;T$, their map is increasing from $[a,\infty)$ onto $[g_s(a),\infty)$. The function $s\mapsto g_s(a)$ is increasing and bounded on $[0,T)$: the driver is bounded there, and once $g_s(a)$ exceeds that bound by one its derivative is at most two. Its limit must therefore be $U_T$, since a different limit would allow the ODE to continue through $T$.</p>

<p>Fix $v&gt;U_T$. For $s&lt;T$ sufficiently close to $T$, the backward flow $h_{s,T}$ from time $T$ to time $s$ is defined in a complex neighbourhood of $v$, is real on the real axis, and satisfies</p>
<div class="math-display">$$h_{s,T}(v)\longrightarrow v&gt;U_T
=\lim_{s\uparrow T}g_s(a).$$</div>
<p>The inverse-flow composition identity, extended to the boundary, consequently gives</p>
<div class="math-display">$$f_T(v)=f_s\bigl(h_{s,T}(v)\bigr)&gt;a.$$</div>
<p>Here $f_s$ agrees with the increasing real inverse on $(g_s(a),\infty)$, by local analytic continuation of the ODE. Letting $v\downarrow U_T$ and using continuity of $f_T$ shows that $X_a=f_T(U_T)$ is real and at least $a$.</p>

<p>A trace visit to a real $x\ne0$ at time $u$ forces $\tau(x)\leq u$. Otherwise the flow extends analytically to a neighbourhood of $x$ through time $u$, with $g_u(x)\ne U_u$. Applying $g_u$ to $f_u(U_u+iy)\to x$ would give $U_u=g_u(x)$, a contradiction. Since $\tau(x)\geq T$ for all $x\geq a$, there is no earlier visit to $[a,\infty)$. The visit to $X_a$ at $T$ gives $\tau(X_a)=T$, and order preservation gives $\tau(b)=T$ for $a\leq b\leq X_a$. Conversely, if $\tau(b)=T$, the argument just given with $b$ in place of $a$ yields $\gamma(T)\geq b$. This proves the lifetime equivalence.</p>

<p>It remains to exclude later visits to $[a,X_a)$. Fix $x$ in this interval. The compact past trace $\gamma[0,T]$ misses $x$, so a small upper half-disk centred at $x$ misses the entire past trace. It belongs to a single component of $\mathbb H\setminus\gamma[0,T]$. Suppose that component were $D_T$. Along its straight real boundary, the local Schwarz reflection theorem extends $g_T$ conformally across $x$, with a real value $v$ and $f_T(v)=x$. The value is finite: hydrodynamic normalization sends infinity to infinity, and the continuous spherical extension of $f_T$ cannot send infinity to $x$.</p>

<p>If $v=U_T$, this contradicts $f_T(U_T)=X_a\ne x$. If $v\ne U_T$, the backward flow exists near $v$ for $s$ close to $T$. Taking boundary limits in its composition identity gives</p>
<div class="math-display">$$g_s(x)=h_{s,T}(v)\longrightarrow v\ne U_T.$$</div>
<p>The real flow would then continue past $T$, contradicting $\tau(x)=T$. Thus the half-disk is disjoint from $D_T$, and $x\notin\overline{D_T}$. Compactness gives $\operatorname{dist}(I,D_T)&gt;0$ for every compact $I\subset[a,X_a)$. Such an $I$ also has positive distance from the compact past trace. Every later tip is a boundary limit of points of $D_u\subset D_T$, so belongs to $\overline{D_T}$. This proves the positive distance from the full trace and identifies its minimum on $[a,\infty)$.</p>

<p>The conformal input in the half-disk argument is local: a conformal map to $\mathbb H$ extends conformally across an open straight boundary segment where its domain lies on one side. It is the usual boundary extension followed by Schwarz reflection. No regularity of the rest of the hull boundary is needed.</p>

## The boundary law and intervals swallowed off the trace {#section-boundary-law}

<p>Now let $U_t=\sqrt\kappa B_t$ with $4&lt;\kappa&lt;8$. The continuous generating trace and boundary extensions used above hold simultaneously at every time by <a href="/tag/MN2A/">MN2A</a>. Set</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\lambda=1-\frac4\kappa,\qquad \mu=\frac8\kappa-1,\qquad
I_r(\lambda,\mu)=\frac1{B(\lambda,\mu)}\int_0^r q^{\lambda-1}(1-q)^{\mu-1}\,dq.$$</div>

<a id="tag-U8XU" data-assertion="theorem" data-title="The SLE boundary beta law holds without an additional geometric comparison and fixed real points are swallowed off the trace"></a>
<p>For each fixed $a&gt;0$, the random variable</p>
<div class="math-display">$$X_a=\min\bigl([a,\infty)\cap\Gamma\bigr)=\gamma(\tau(a))$$</div>
<p>is finite, is strictly greater than $a$ almost surely, and satisfies</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\mathbb P(X_a\geq b)=I_{a/b}(\lambda,\mu),\qquad b\geq a.$$</div>
<p>Equivalently, $a/X_a$ has the beta distribution with parameters $\lambda,\mu$. Every fixed real $x\ne0$ has finite lifetime and positive distance from $\Gamma$ almost surely. Moreover, for every compact interval $I\subset\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$,</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\mathbb P\bigl(\exists t&lt;\infty:\operatorname{dist}(I,D_t)&gt;0\bigr)&gt;0.$$</div>

<p><em>Proof.</em> The lifetime of $a$ is finite by <a href="/tag/6DV8/">6DV8</a>. For each $b&gt;a$, E5JX and the simultaneous-swallowing formula <a href="/tag/KQIN/">KQIN</a> give</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\mathbb P(X_a\geq b)
=\mathbb P\bigl(\tau(a)=\tau(b)\bigr)
=I_{a/b}(\lambda,\mu).$$</div>
<p>Both beta parameters are positive. The tail is continuous for $b&gt;a$ and tends to one as $b\downarrow a$, so $X_a$ has no atoms there and $\mathbb P(X_a&gt;a)=1$. Taking $I=\{a\}$ in E5JX proves positive distance of $a$ from the full trace. Reflection gives the same statement at each fixed negative point. These are fixed-point assertions; the random boundary points visited by the trace are not excluded.</p>

<p>For an interval $I=[a,b]$ with $0&lt;a\leq b$, choose $c&gt;b$. On $\{X_a\geq c\}$ the interval is a compact subset of $[a,X_a)$, so E5JX gives $\operatorname{dist}(I,D_{\tau(a)})&gt;0$. This event has probability $I_{a/c}(\lambda,\mu)&gt;0$. Reflection handles negative intervals. The event in the statement is measurable: by domain nesting it is the union over integer $t$, and distance to the open domain can be computed using its points with rational coordinates.</p>

<p>For $a=1$ this proves the geometric input retained in <a href="/tag/2L0Z/">2L0Z</a>. Indeed, E5JX identifies $X_1\geq b$ with equal swallowing times, while KQIN classifies the ratio limit as one or zero according to equality or strict inequality of those times. The earlier hypergeometric expression is therefore the unconditional trace law in this range. The fixed-real-point conclusion, together with <a href="/tag/O0MI/">the interior-point result</a>, supplies the intermediate-regime assertion of Rohde–Schramm Theorem 6.4.</p>

## A neighbourhood of the origin disappears immediately {#section-origin-engulfment}

<a id="tag-ZI7H" data-assertion="theorem" data-title="For kappa between four and eight the Loewner domain is separated from the origin at every positive time"></a>
<p>For $4&lt;\kappa&lt;8$, almost surely, simultaneously for every $t&gt;0$,</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\rho_t:=\operatorname{dist}(0,D_t)&gt;0.$$</div>
<p>Thus the hull $K_t=\overline{\mathbb H\setminus D_t}$ contains the upper half-disk $B(0,\rho_t)\cap\mathbb H$.</p>

<p><em>Proof.</em> Stop at $T=\tau(1)$, which is finite almost surely, and consider the boundary preimages of the origin:</p>
<div class="math-display">$$S=\{v\in\mathbb R:f_T(v)=0\}.$$</div>
<p>This is a compact set, possibly empty: it is closed by boundary continuity, and hydrodynamic normalization excludes preimages escaping to infinity. The proof of E5JX gives $f_T(v)&gt;1$ for $v&gt;U_T$ and $f_T(U_T)=\gamma(T)\geq1$. Consequently $S-U_T$ is a compact subset of $(-\infty,0)$.</p>

<p>The events</p>
<div class="math-display">$$A_N=\{S-U_T\subset(-N,-1/N)\},\qquad N\geq2,$$</div>
<p>cover a probability-one event. They belong to $\mathcal F_T$: extend $v\mapsto f_T(v+U_T)$ continuously to the real circle, including infinity. Then $A_N$ says that its chordal distance from zero has a positive minimum on the compact complement of $(-N,-1/N)$. This minimum is the infimum over a fixed countable dense subset, and the boundary values are limits of the measurable interior values. Choose a deterministic $N$ with $\mathbb P(A_N)&gt;0$.</p>

<p>By the Loewner composition rule and Brownian strong Markov property, the future domains in the coordinates</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\widetilde D_u=g_T(D_{T+u})-U_T$$</div>
<p>form an independent fresh SLE chain. U8XU gives a probability $p_N&gt;0$ that, at some finite time $u$, this chain has</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\operatorname{dist}\bigl([-N,-1/N],\widetilde D_u\bigr)&gt;0.$$</div>
<p>The intersection of this event with $A_N$ has probability $p_N\mathbb P(A_N)&gt;0$. On that intersection the original domain at time $T+u$ is separated from zero. To see this, suppose $z_j\in D_{T+u}$ tended to zero and set $w_j=g_T(z_j)-U_T\in\widetilde D_u$. The sequence $w_j$ is bounded, since $f_T(w+U_T)\to\infty$ as $w\to\infty$ in the closed half-plane. After taking a subsequence, $w_j\to w\in\overline{\mathbb H}$ and continuity gives $f_T(w+U_T)=0$. An interior preimage is impossible, so $w\in S-U_T\subset[-N,-1/N]$, contradicting the displayed positive distance.</p>

<p>We have proved that the event $E_t=\{\rho_t&gt;0\}$ occurs at some finite time with positive probability. These events are measurable with respect to $\mathcal F_t$ and increase with $t$. Scaling makes $\mathbb P(E_t)=\delta$ independent of $t&gt;0$, and the union over integer times shows that $\delta&gt;0$. The decreasing intersection $\bigcap_{n\geq1}E_{1/n}$ has probability $\delta$ and belongs to the Brownian germ sigma-field. Blumenthal's zero-one law gives $\delta=1$. For any positive $t$, some $1/n&lt;t$; monotonicity now gives $E_t$ on this single probability-one event.</p>

## Transience and simultaneous finite lifetimes {#section-transience}

<a id="tag-G09W" data-assertion="theorem" data-title="SLE between four and eight is transient and its hulls cover every compact half-plane set in finite time"></a>
<p>For $4&lt;\kappa&lt;8$, almost surely</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\lim_{t\to\infty}\rho_t=\infty,\qquad
\lim_{t\to\infty}|\gamma(t)|=\infty.$$</div>
<p>On the same event, every compact $C\subset\overline{\mathbb H}$ is contained in a finite-time hull, and $\tau(z)&lt;\infty$ simultaneously for every $z\in\overline{\mathbb H}\setminus\{0\}$.</p>

<p><em>Proof.</em> The distances $\rho_t$ increase, and Brownian scaling gives $\rho_t\overset{\mathrm{law}}=\sqrt t\,\rho_1$. Since $\rho_1&gt;0$ almost surely by ZI7H, for each $R&gt;0$,</p>
<div class="math-display">$$\mathbb P(\rho_t\leq R)
=\mathbb P(\rho_1\leq R/\sqrt t)\longrightarrow0.$$</div>
<p>Monotonicity and a countable intersection over positive integer $R$ give $\rho_t\to\infty$ almost surely. Every future tip $\gamma(s)$, $s\geq t$, belongs to $\overline{D_t}$, so $|\gamma(s)|\geq\rho_t$. This proves transience.</p>

<p>For any $R&lt;\rho_T$, the open upper half-disk of radius $\rho_T$ is disjoint from $D_T$. Its closure contains the closed half-disk of radius $R$, so that half-disk lies in $K_T$. This proves finite-time coverage of all compact sets, pathwise. An interior point outside $D_T$ has lifetime at most $T$. If a real $x\ne0$ with $|x|&lt;\rho_T$ had $\tau(x)&gt;T$, local analytic continuation of its real flow would put an upper neighbourhood of $x$ in $D_T$, contradicting the definition of $\rho_T$. Thus its lifetime is at most $T$ as well. Increasing integer radii now give the simultaneous assertion without intersecting uncountably many fixed-point probability statements.</p>

<p>Compact coverage here is by the hulls. The trace still has zero planar area by <a href="/tag/NHWR/">NHWR</a>. The three SLE consequences above supply the remaining intermediate-regime parts of Rohde–Schramm Theorem 6.4, Lemma 6.6, Lemma 7.3 and Theorem 7.1, with the stronger quantifier noted after Lemma 6.5. Their statements and proofs were checked in <a href="https://arxiv.org/html/math/0106036v4">version 4, Sections 6–7</a>. The local boundary argument above replaces the extremal-distance step; the independent-future argument spells out the compact preimage set in the origin-engulfment proof. No new source error is asserted. The kappa-eight trace theorem and the boundary-area input for the regime above eight remain separate.</p>
