# Cited inputs

D-4 register of every `…Input` hypothesis structure in the library: name, file, the
literature result it stands for, and the exact locator. A target that uses one is
`proved-modulo`, never `proved`.

As of 20 November 2026 (integration tip `eb09ac5`) the library has **no** `…Input`
structure; a search for `Input` in `PercLib/` finds none. Conditional results take
ordinary named hypotheses instead and are reported as conditional, not as proved
theorems of their source.
